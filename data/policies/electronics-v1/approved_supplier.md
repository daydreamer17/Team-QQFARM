# Fictional Approved Supplier Policy

This synthetic policy exists only for the NUS-ISS hackathon demonstration.

## [ASL-001] Registry evidence
Approved supplier status must be supported by the current supplier registry record at the evaluation time.

## [ASL-002] Exact identity matching
Supplier approval must be checked using the confirmed supplier identifier. Policy-text similarity must not be used to infer or repair supplier identity.

## [ASL-003] Inactive supplier
A supplier whose registry status is inactive is not eligible for an approved-supplier recommendation.

## [ASL-004] Expired or missing approval
An expired approval period or missing registry record requires procurement review and must not be treated as approved.
