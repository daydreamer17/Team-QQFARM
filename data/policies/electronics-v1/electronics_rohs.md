# Fictional Electronics RoHS Evidence Policy

This synthetic policy exists only for the NUS-ISS hackathon demonstration.

## [ROHS-001] Electronics evidence requirement
Electronics purchases require current RoHS evidence for each compared part before a supplier can be marked compliant.

## [ROHS-002] Accepted evidence
Accepted evidence is a signed supplier declaration or a recognized certificate that states the applicable RoHS scope and issue date.

## [ROHS-003] Evidence identity
The supplier identifier and part identifier on RoHS evidence must match the confirmed supplier and requested part records.

## [ROHS-004] Missing or expired evidence
Missing, expired, or mismatched RoHS evidence requires review. Retrieval similarity alone cannot establish RoHS compliance.
