## [CCD-SUP-001] Supplier admission

A supplier must have a human-confirmed admission record before it is eligible for a verified recommendation. Match the supplier ID to the quotation. A declaration of refused admission fails the requirement. Missing, expired, mismatched or conflicting records require review. The record must state its validity period or explicitly state permanent validity; an unspecified period is insufficient. The system checks confirmed facts and does not authenticate the source.
