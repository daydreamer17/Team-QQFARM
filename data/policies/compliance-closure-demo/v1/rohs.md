## [CCD-ROHS-001] RoHS material coverage

Before a verified recommendation, obtain a human-confirmed RoHS declaration that covers the quotation supplier, manufacturer and exact manufacturer part number. The declaration must state conformity and have an explicit validity period or explicit permanent validity. A declaration of non-conformity fails the requirement. Missing, expired, mismatched or conflicting evidence requires review. A declaration for a different part number does not cover the purchased part.
