## [CCD-AMT-001] Post-selection amount action

After selection, a proposal with a confirmed total procurement cost greater than or equal to SGD 10000.00 requires the buyer to obtain the organisation's separate amount approval before ordering. The total-cost basis includes the confirmed quantity, goods and applicable confirmed charges calculated by the procurement comparison rules. Do not convert other currencies or treat unknown charges as zero. An unsupported currency or unknown total requires review. This is an AFTER_SELECTION action: it does not certify supplier qualifications and does not block comparison publication. The application records the required action but does not grant or execute approval. Falling below the threshold is not purchase authorisation. These rules are fictional demonstration requirements.
