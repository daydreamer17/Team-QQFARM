## [CCD2-AMT-001] Approval before publication

A supplier proposal with confirmed total procurement cost greater than or equal to SGD 6500.00 requires a recorded amount approval before publication. The record must match the quotation supplier, be effective at evaluation time, state SGD, show an APPROVED decision, and approve at least the proposal total. An insufficient ceiling, other currency, expired record, rejected decision or conflicting record does not satisfy the condition. The application records and verifies supplied approval facts; it does not grant approval.
