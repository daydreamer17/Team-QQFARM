## [CCD2-SUP-001] Supplier admission and adverse status

Before recommendation, a supplier must have a human-confirmed admission record whose supplier ID matches the quotation and whose scope includes electronics procurement in Singapore. APPROVED passes. SUSPENDED, REVOKED or REFUSED fails. Missing, expired, future-effective, mismatched or conflicting records require review. The record must state a validity period or explicit permanent validity. The application checks confirmed facts and does not authenticate the source.
