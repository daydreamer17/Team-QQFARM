# Fictional Quote Change and Re-review Policy

This synthetic policy exists only for the NUS-ISS hackathon demonstration.

## [CHG-001] Price changes
A change to unit price, currency, quantity, or discount creates a new quote version and requires extraction, review, and comparison to run again.

## [CHG-002] Delivery and charge changes
A change to shipping, mandatory charges, minimum order quantity, or lead time requires a new task revision and re-review.

## [CHG-003] Administrative corrections
Administrative corrections may leave totals unchanged but must record the actor and correction. Corrections affecting supplier identity or evidence linkage require a new task revision and reassessment.

## [CHG-004] Superseded results
Results from an earlier task revision remain immutable history and must not overwrite the current result after a quote change.
