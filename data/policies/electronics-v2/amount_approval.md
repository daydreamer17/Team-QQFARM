# Fictional Procurement Amount Approval Policy

This synthetic policy exists only for the NUS-ISS hackathon demonstration.

## [APR-001] Approval threshold
A proposed award with total landed cost of SGD 10,000 or more requires manager approval before an effective approved final report is published. Awards below this threshold still require ordinary human approval. Supplier compliance is not approval.

## [APR-002] Threshold basis
The approval threshold is evaluated against total landed cost after required charges and approved currency conversion have been applied.

## [APR-003] Approval evidence
The approving manager, approval timestamp, approved amount, and related task revision must be recorded as evidence.

## [APR-004] Foreign currency evaluation
A foreign-currency award must use the approved exchange-rate snapshot effective at the evaluation timestamp when testing the SGD approval threshold.
