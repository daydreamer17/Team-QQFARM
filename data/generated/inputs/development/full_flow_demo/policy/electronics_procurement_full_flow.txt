# Fictional Full Flow Electronics Release Gate Policy

This synthetic policy exists only for the QuoteWise demonstration.
It is not a real procurement policy and grants no purchasing authority.

## [ASL-001] Registry evidence
Approved supplier status must be supported by the current supplier registry record at the evaluation time.

## [ASL-002] Exact identity matching
Supplier approval must be checked using the confirmed supplier identifier. Policy-text similarity must not be used to infer or repair supplier identity.

## [ASL-003] Inactive supplier
A supplier whose registry status is inactive is not eligible for an approved-supplier recommendation.

## [ASL-004] Expired or missing approval
An expired approval period or missing registry record requires procurement review and must not be treated as approved.

## [ROHS-001] Electronics evidence requirement
Electronics purchases require current RoHS evidence for each compared part before a supplier can be marked compliant.

## [ROHS-002] Accepted evidence
Accepted evidence is a signed supplier declaration or a recognized certificate that states the applicable RoHS scope and issue date.

## [ROHS-003] Evidence identity
The supplier identifier and part identifier on RoHS evidence must match the confirmed supplier and requested part records.

## [ROHS-004] Missing or expired evidence
Missing, expired, or mismatched RoHS evidence requires review. Retrieval similarity alone cannot establish RoHS compliance.

## [APR-001] Approval threshold
A proposed award with total landed cost of SGD 10,000 or more requires manager approval before publication.

## [APR-002] Threshold basis
The approval threshold is evaluated against total landed cost after required charges and approved currency conversion have been applied.

## [APR-003] Approval evidence
The approving manager, approval timestamp, approved amount, and related task revision must be recorded as evidence.

## [APR-004] Foreign currency evaluation
A foreign-currency award must use the approved exchange-rate snapshot effective at the evaluation timestamp when testing the SGD approval threshold.
