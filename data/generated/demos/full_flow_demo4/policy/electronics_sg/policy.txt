# SYNTHETIC procurement policy

## [FF4-ASL] Supplier identity evidence
For SG electronics procurement, approved supplier status requires an exact supplier ID and a current registry record. A quotation or a similar supplier name alone is insufficient.

## [FF4-ROHS] Part-level compliance evidence
For SG electronics procurement, RoHS compliance requires valid evidence for the offered manufacturer part number. Missing evidence remains REVIEW_REQUIRED, not compliant and not automatically rejected.

## [FF4-APP] Amount approval threshold
For SG electronics procurement, a selected quotation with confirmed total cost of SGD 7000.00 or more requires recorded approval after selection. A model recommendation is not approval.
